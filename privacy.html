Privacy policy

How we use your information.

Short version: we use it only to talk with you about your project, and we never sell it.

What we collect

When you fill out our estimate form, we receive what you type: your name, phone number, email (if you give it), town, the kind of project and your description. When you call or text us, we have your phone number and what you tell us.

How we use it

Only to contact you about your project: to answer your questions, set up a visit and give you a written estimate. We may call or text the phone number you give us. If you would rather we only call, or would like us to stop contacting you, just tell us and we will.

Who else sees it

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. Our estimate form is delivered to us by Formspree, a service that forwards form messages to our email. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts. Like any website, these services may receive basic technical information such as your IP address when you visit.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking tools.

How long we keep it

We keep your information as long as we need it for your project and our business records. To ask us to delete it, call us at (240) 462-1150.
